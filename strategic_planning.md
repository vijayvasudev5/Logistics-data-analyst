# Strategic Planning

## 1. Logistics Scenario
A logistics company receives customer orders and transports products from warehouses to different destinations. The company wants to improve delivery reliability while controlling transportation costs.

## 2. Business Problem
The company needs to understand whether orders are being delivered on time, how much transportation costs, which routes experience delays, and how distance affects delivery time and cost.

## 3. Data Science Approach
The project uses exploratory data analysis (EDA) with Python. The dataset is loaded using pandas. Basic statistics, KPI calculations, grouping, and visualizations are used to understand operational performance.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators
- **On-Time Delivery Rate:** percentage of orders delivered on or before the promised delivery time.
- **Delay Rate:** percentage of orders delivered after the promised delivery time.
- **Average Delivery Time:** average number of days required for delivery.
- **Average Transportation Cost:** average cost per order.
- **Total Logistics Cost:** total transportation cost for all orders.
- **Average Distance:** average transportation distance.

## 5. Strategic Use of Data
The results can support:
- Better route planning.
- Identification of frequently delayed routes.
- Transportation cost monitoring.
- Warehouse and vehicle resource allocation.
- Delivery performance monitoring.
- Future demand forecasting.

## 6. Future Scope
Future versions can include real-time GPS data, weather information, traffic conditions, machine learning-based delay prediction, demand forecasting, and an interactive dashboard using Power BI or Flask.
